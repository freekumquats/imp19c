# Late-Qing / Meiji Character Roster — Research Digest

Roster of ~50 late-Qing/Meiji historical figures (stats, traits, mechanic hook) for the anachronistic-spawn feature (task #13). Figures spawn ANACHRONISTICALLY, keyed to mechanic context not birth date. Stats are Imperator 4-axis: Martial / Finesse / Charisma / Zeal (0-9 scale here; scale to engine at build). Sources = EN Wikipedia (ZH primary detail in RESEARCH_GRAND_COUNCIL_OFFICES.md). Distinct from QIANLONG_ROSTER_RESEARCH.md (that is the peak-Qing 1759/1790 bench).

## GRAND COUNCILLORS / HIGH OFFICIALS
- **和珅 Heshen** (1750-99) F9 C7 Z3 M2 — corrupt/greedy/brilliant-admin/nepotist. Board of Revenue / chief councillor; drains treasury, props court stability while alive, anti-corruption events on purge. **Decline-track anchor.**
- **阿桂 Agui** (1717-97) M8 F7 Z5 C5 — competent-cmdr/logistician/stubborn. Grand councillor or frontier cmdr.
- **劉墉 Liu Yong** (1719-1805) F8 Z7 C5 M2 — incorruptible/upright/scholar. Anti-corruption + council.
- **恭親王奕訢 Prince Gong / Yixin** (1833-98) F9 C7 Z6 M5 — reformer/diplomat/brilliant-admin/pragmatic. Head of Zongli Yamen; **Self-Strengthening + diplomacy patron.**
- **文祥 Wenxiang** (d.1876) F7 Z6 C5 M4 — reformer/admin/loyal. Zongli Yamen co-founder.
- **榮祿 Ronglu** (1836-1903) M6 F7 C5 Z4 — conservative/loyal/pragmatic. Min. of War, Wuwei Corps. **Anti-reform faction anchor** (backed 1898 coup).
- **翁同龢 Weng Tonghe** (1830-1904) F7 Z6 C4 M1 — scholar/cautious/conservative-reformer. Imperial tutor; cut navy funding.
- **肅順 Sushun** (1816-61) F6 C4 Z5 M3 — ambitious/traditionalist/doomed. Regency-council; triggers coup if emperor dies young.
- **奕劻 Prince Qing / Yikuang** (1838-1917) F5 C3 Z2 M2 — corrupt/weak/foreign-friendly. Last chief councillor → 1911 Imperial Cabinet PM.
- **奕譞 Prince Chun / Yixuan** (1840-91) F6 Z5 C3 M4 — cautious/self-effacing/paranoid. Admiralty head; Beiyang fleet events.
- **載灃 Prince Regent Zaifeng** (1883-1951) F4 Z4 C3 M2 — ineffectual/well-meaning/overwhelmed. Endgame regent; abolishes Grand Council → Imperial Cabinet 1911.

## SELF-STRENGTHENING STATESMEN-GENERALS
- **曾國藩 Zeng Guofan** (1811-72) M8 F8 Z7 C6 — brilliant-admin/ruthless/scholar/mentor. Xiang Army 湘軍, Shanghai Arsenal.
- **李鴻章 Li Hongzhang** (1823-1901) F9 M7 C7 Z6 — "Oriental Bismarck"; brilliant-admin/diplomat/progressive/pragmatic. Huai Army 淮軍, Beiyang Fleet; **~90% of SS projects; SS hub + diplomacy.**
- **左宗棠 Zuo Zongtang** (1812-85) M9 F7 Z8 C6 — stubborn/brilliant-cmdr/reformer/frontier. Foochow Arsenal, Xinjiang reconquest 1878.
- **張之洞 Zhang Zhidong** (1837-1909) F8 Z7 C6 M5 — reformer/pragmatic/conservative-modernizer. Hanyang Ironworks; ti-yong; led 1905 exam abolition.
- **沈葆楨 Shen Baozhen** (1820-79) F7 M6 Z6 C5 — admin/reformer/rail-cautious. Foochow naval school, Taiwan.
- **丁日昌 Ding Richang** (1823-82) F7 Z8 C4 M5 — reformer/ambitious/impetuous/upright. Kiangnan Arsenal, first telegraph.
- **袁世凱 Yuan Shikai** (1859-1916) M8 F8 C6 Z5 — ambitious/pragmatic/betrayer/brilliant-cmdr. Beiyang Army (Xiaozhan New Army); betrays 1898 reformers; 1911 → president → self-proclaimed emperor. **Can betray player.**
- **曾國荃 Zeng Guoquan** (1824-90) M8 F5 Z6 C3 — ruthless/siege-expert/brutal. Captured Nanjing 1864; atrocity events.

## REFORMERS & REVOLUTIONARIES (reform-track drivers)
- **林則徐 Lin Zexu** (1785-1850) Z9 F7 C6 M4 — incorruptible/patriot/rigid/moralist. Humen opium destruction 1839; opium-ban + First Opium War trigger.
- **魏源 Wei Yuan** (1794-1857) F8 Z8 C5 M2 — scholar/reformer/visionary. 海國圖志; "師夷之長技以制夷". Unlocks SS ideology (early).
- **馮桂芬 Feng Guifen** (1809-74) F8 Z7 C5 M2 — scholar/reformer/pragmatic/confucian. Coined self-strengthening framing; ti-yong originator.
- **康有為 Kang Youwei** (1858-1927) F9 Z9 C7 M1 — reformer/visionary/radical/messianic. **Constitutional-monarchy path; HIGH anachronism.** Hundred Days' Reform.
- **梁啟超 Liang Qichao** (1873-1929) F9 C8 Z8 M1 — reformer/brilliant-writer/progressive/nationalist. **Constitutional path + media/opinion layer; HIGH anachronism.**
- **譚嗣同 Tan Sitong** (c.1865-98) Z9 C6 F5 M3 — reformer/martyr/idealist/brave. 戊戌六君子; 1898 martyrdom.
- **孫中山 Sun Yat-sen** (1866-1925) C9 Z9 F6 M4 — revolutionary/visionary/persistent/nationalist. Tongmenghui/KMT; 三民主義. **Republican path + revolution crisis; HIGHEST anachronism.**
- **黃興 Huang Xing** (1874-1916) M8 Z8 C6 F5 — revolutionary/military-leader/brave. Tongmenghui military wing. HIGH anachronism.
- **宋教仁 Song Jiaoren** (1882-1913) F8 C7 Z7 M2 — revolutionary/organizer/arrogant/democratic. KMT parliamentary victory 1912; assassinated by Yuan. HIGH anachronism.
- **鄒容 Zou Rong** (1885-1905) F7 Z9 C6 M1 — revolutionary/writer/radical/anti-Manchu. 革命軍 1903. HIGH anachronism.
- **秋瑾 Qiu Jin** (1875-1907) Z9 C7 M6 F5 — revolutionary/feminist/brave/martyr. HIGH anachronism.
