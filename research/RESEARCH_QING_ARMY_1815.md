# Qing Dual Standing Army c.1815 — Research Digest

Backing **task #66** (dual Eight Banners + Green Standard garrison system). Grounded in standard historiography (Elliott *The Manchu Way*; Luo Ergang 羅爾綱 *綠營兵志 Lüying bingzhi*; Perdue *China Marches West*; Millward *Beyond the Pass*; Yingcong Dai *The Sichuan Frontier and Tibet*; Cambridge History of China v.9–10; Wei Yuan 聖武記) — internal search tools only surfaced Wikipedia mirrors, so treat exact digits as scholarly-consensus estimates, not archival.

**⚠ DATA-QUALITY TRAP (caught in review):** the supporting extraction agents produced "1885" provincial figures in the MILLIONS (e.g. Xinjiang 1,568,039; Zhili 1,321,953). These are **military-expenditure-in-taels** columns from a late-Qing budget table, mis-read as troop counts. NO Qing province had a million soldiers. DISCARD them.

## The dual standing army (the key structural fact)
Two permanent, separately-administered, ethnically-defined institutions, meeting only at the throne:
- **Eight Banners 八旗 ~250,000 SOLDIERS** c.1800–1820 (adult male soldiers on the rolls; total banner *population* incl. dependents was ~1.5–2M — do not conflate). Hereditary conquest-elite caste + STRATEGIC RESERVE, not a routine constabulary. 24 banners = three parallel 8-banner wings: **Manchu 滿洲八旗** (core), **Mongol 蒙古八旗** (cavalry-heavy, split out 1635), **Han-martial 漢軍八旗** (artillery; SHRINKING by 1815 via 出旗 chuqi purges). Basic unit = company 佐領/niru (~nominal 300).
- **Green Standard 綠營 ~600,000** (paper/establishment strength, mid-Qing consensus; Luo Ergang). Han professional TERRITORIAL/CONSTABULARY army — infantry/cavalry/marine dispersed in tiny posts (汛 xun, 塘 tang) across every province for policing, escort, coast/canal patrol, first-line suppression. DELIBERATELY fragmented (anti-warlordism) → model as many small stacks, never one concentration.

## Metropolitan vs provincial banners (map placement)
- **禁旅八旗 jinlü baqi — metropolitan/capital banners: ~half of all banner soldiers (~100,000+) at/around BEIJING.** Includes guard divisions: vanguard 前鋒營, guards 護軍營, Firearms/Artillery 火器營, Light Division 健銳營, and the Gendarmerie/Nine-Gates command 步軍統領衙門. Plus tomb/palace guards.
- **駐防八旗 zhufang baqi — provincial garrison banners: the remainder (~100,000–120,000)** in ~20 walled "Manchu cities" 滿城 mancheng.

## Banner garrison cities (drop-points for #66; soldier establishments)
- **Capital:** Beijing (~100,000+, by far the largest).
- **Manchuria homeland (3 military governors):** Mukden/Shengjing 盛京 (Shenyang), Jilin 吉林, Heilongjiang 黑龍江 (seats Qiqihar/Aigun/Mergen) — tens of thousands combined, thin frontier force facing Russia.
- **Xinjiang/NW (post-1759):** **Ili 伊犁** (seat of the 伊犁將軍, supreme governor of Xinjiang — largest western garrison + tuntian colonies); subord. Ürümqi/Dihua 烏魯木齊, Tarbagatai/Tacheng 塔爾巴哈台, Kashgar 喀什噶爾.
- **Interior mancheng (~1,500–4,000 each):** **Xi'an 西安** (largest interior), Jingzhou 荊州 (~1,600, mid-Yangzi choke), Hangzhou 杭州 (~1,600), Jiangning/Nanjing 江寧 + river garrison Jingkou/Zhenjiang 京口, Chengdu 成都, Canton/Guangzhou 廣州 (coastal, later reinforced), Ningxia 寧夏 (~3,600), Liangzhou 涼州 (~2,700), Fuzhou 福州, Zhapu 乍浦 (Zhejiang coast), Qingzhou 青州 (Shandong), Kaifeng 開封, Taiyuan 太原, Tongguan 潼關, Suiyuan/Guihua 綏遠·歸化 (Inner Mongolia).

## Green Standard heavy concentrations (by 總督 region)
Zhili 直隸 (guards Beijing approaches), Liangjiang 兩江 (rich lower-Yangzi), Huguang 湖廣, **Sichuan 四川** (logistical spine of Tibet/frontier + White Lotus theatre), Liangguang 兩廣 (coast/anti-piracy + 水師 marine squadrons), Shaan-Gan 陝甘 (Muslim frontier), Fujian 福建 (incl. **Taiwan 臺灣** garrison + Straits marine), Yunnan-Guizhou 雲貴 (SW tribal frontier, post-gaitu-guiliu).

## Command structure (two parallel chains)
- **Banner:** 將軍 jiāngjūn ("Tartar General") = banner military governor of a garrison city/region (Ili General, Shengjing General, Xi'an General, Canton General…), always a bannerman; under him 副都統 fu dutong + 城守尉 commandants.
- **Green Standard/provincial:** 總督 zongdu (viceroy, over ~2 provinces, own escort 督標) > 巡撫 xunfu (provincial governor, guard 撫標) > **提督 tidu** (provincial commander-in-chief, land 陸路 + coastal naval 水師) > **總兵 zongbing** (brigade-general commanding a 鎮 zhen) > 副將(協)/參將/遊擊/都司/守備 > 千總/把總.
- **Named commanders c.1815:** Nayancheng 那彥成 (char:358 already used as Green Standard commander in SE_qing_armies), Changling 長齡, **Yang Yuchun 楊遇春** (outstanding *Han* Green Standard general, White Lotus + Jahangir — emblem that Green Standard/Han won the wars), Eldemboo 額勒登保, Delengtai 德楞泰.

## State of decay by 1815 → START QUALITY = big-on-paper, brittle-in-field
- **旗人生計 banner-poverty crisis:** bannermen barred from trades, fixed stipends eroded by inflation, population outgrew soldier slots → mass of impoverished, militarily-soft bannermen. Court legislated repeatedly (debt relief, Hanjun purges, failed resettlement).
- **Sinicization / lost 國語騎射** (Manchu language + mounted archery) — the emperors' repeated preservation edicts are themselves proof of decay.
- **White Lotus War 川楚白蓮教亂 1796–1804** = the demonstration: regulars performed poorly; won by locally-raised **militia 鄉勇 xiangyong / 團練 tuanlian** under Han commanders (Yang Yuchun) + 堅壁清野 "strengthen walls, clear countryside." Banners couldn't project force. Direct ancestor of the later Xiang Army vs Taiping.
- **1813 Eight Trigrams 天理教/八卦教 uprising** penetrated the Forbidden City itself — rot in capital security.
- **Paper vs real:** 250k/600k are NOMINAL rolls; effective strength materially lower (desertion, "ghost soldiers," obsolete matchlocks, embezzled provisions, Heshen-era 和珅 financial rot). Treat paper numbers as CEILINGS.
