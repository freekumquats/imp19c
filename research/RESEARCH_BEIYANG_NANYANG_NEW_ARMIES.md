# Late-Qing Modern Armies (Beiyang 北洋 / Nanyang New Armies) — Research Digest

Backing **#95** (Beiyang/Nanyang modern-ARMY missions/events, complementing the existing Beiyang/Nanyang modern-NAVY content). Sources: encyclopedic, cross-checked vs Powell/MacKinnon/Fung/van de Ven, 來新夏《北洋軍閥史》, 《清史稿·兵志》. Figures flagged approximate where sources vary.

**Beiyang Army 北洋新軍 lineage**: 1895 Dingwu Army 定武軍 at 小站 Xiaozhan (10 營/~4,750 men, Hu Yufen 胡燏棻 + German von Hanneken) → 8 Dec 1895 Yuan Shikai 袁世凱 takes command, renames 新建陸軍 Xinjian Lujun (~7,000, German model) → 1898 folded into 武衛軍 Wuwei Corps as Right Division (武衛右軍) under Ronglu 榮祿; survives 1900 Boxer intact → 1902 renamed Beiyang Army on Yuan's promotion to 北洋大臣 → 1902-05 consolidated into 北洋六鎮 Six Divisions (1st=Manchu bannermen, 4th=original Newly Created core, 6th=Wuwei Left + Self-Strengthening Army elements). ~20k (1902) → ~60k (1907) → 75k+ (1911, ~2/3 of nominal national army).

**Org ladder (陸軍 standard, set by 練兵處 1904)**: 鎮 zhen (division, 12,500) › 協 xie (brigade ~3,024) › 標 biao (regiment 1,512) › 營 ying (battalion 504) › 隊 dui (company 168) › 排 pai (platoon 42). Army/corps above: 大軍 dajun › 軍 jun.

**Nanyang / Self-Strengthening Army 自強軍**: 1895 Nanjing (兩江), Zhang Zhidong 張之洞, ~10k German-drilled proposal (~35 German officers). Transferred to Liu Kunyi 劉坤一 1896 → stagnated (never expanded), politically orphaned, elements absorbed into Beiyang 6th Division. Zhang's real modern-army legacy shifted to Hubei/Wuchang (~11,500 by 1904) — which ironically fired the 1911 Wuchang Uprising.

**New Army 新軍 program**: 1901 abolished military exam; 1903 edict planned 36 divisions × 12,500 = ~450k (target 1922→1913); NEVER exceeded ~300k (key plan-vs-reality gap). 練兵處 Commission (1903-04): director Prince Qing 慶親王, assistants Yuan + Tieliang 鐵良 (Manchu counterweight → Yuan's 1906/1909 clawbacks).

**Lineage arc (game-usable)**: 勇營 yongying (Xiang/Huai) → 淮軍 Huai Army (Li Hongzhang, 1861) → 北洋新軍 Beiyang. Personnel continuity Li→Yuan direct (Yuan listed among Huai secondary leaders). Ever-Victorious Army = earlier Western-drill precedent. New Army superseded yongying/Green Standard → downgraded to 巡防隊 Xunfangdui garrison reserves (max 50 bn/province) until 1911.

**Warlord seed (delayed payload)**: Yuan's personally-loyal Xiaozhan/保定 Baoding Academy (1902) officers — Duan Qirui 段祺瑞, Feng Guozhang 馮國璋, Cao Kun 曹錕, Wu Peifu 吳佩孚, Zhang Zuolin 張作霖 → after Yuan's death (1916) fracture into Anhui 皖系 / Zhili 直系 / Fengtian 奉系 cliques.

**Mission/event hooks**: era-gate ~1895+; two-track program (North=Beiyang/Yuan dominant vs South=Nanyang/Zhang orphaned) as competing reform projects; Six Divisions as spawnable modern-army units (route to existing qing_ever_victorious or a new modern-army type); 36-division plan as aspirational-but-underdelivered goal; warlord-clique split as delayed civil-war seed (couples to existing grandee/qing_yongying loyal-cohort mechanic in se_QING_COUNCIL).
