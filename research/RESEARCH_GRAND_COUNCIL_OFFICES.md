# Grand Council / Offices / Self-Strengthening / Diplomacy — Research Digest

Backs the Grand Council / offices / Self-Strengthening / diplomacy feature wave (2026-07-04 research reports). Design-load-bearing facts + citations.

## GRAND COUNCIL 軍機處 (Jūnjī Chù)
- Est. Yongzheng: Military Needs Office 軍需房 1729 → 軍機處 1730 → formalized 辦理軍機處 1732 (Dzungar war secrecy). Abolished May 1911 → 責任內閣 (Imperial/"royal" Cabinet, Yikuang, 13 members, Manchu-dominated) → Xinhai.
- Composition: 軍機大臣 (grand councillors, "大軍機") NO fixed number, ~4-7, drawn from princes/grand secretaries/ministers; designation 軍機處行走. Chief = 領班軍機大臣. Secretaries 軍機章京 ("小軍機"), Manchu-Han equal split (滿漢各半), 4 shifts of 32 by 1799.
- Powers: draft edicts 撰擬諭旨, process palace memorials 奏摺, issue court letters 廷寄/字寄 (bypass the Cabinet), 議大政/讞大獄, appointments. Secrecy = "官而無吏"; no unauthorized entry. Concentrated power in emperor's hands, eclipsing 議政王大臣會議 (Deliberative Council, abolished practice), 南書房, and 內閣 (Grand Secretariat, left w/ ticket-drafting 票擬).
- 19C: princes admitted 1853 (Prince Gong 恭親王奕訢); under dowagers Ci'an/Cixi post-1861 papers → dowagers → Prince Gong. Zeng Guofan 1869: councillors' power "surpassed the imperial master." Jiaqing 1799 reforms after purging Heshen (和珅, chief councillor 1797-99).
- Primary sources: 樞垣記略 (梁章鉅, ex-secretary, vol 27), 清實錄, 清史稿·職官志, 檐曝雜記 (趙翼), 內閣小志 (葉鳳毛). Secondary: Bartlett *Monarchs and Ministers* (1991), Ho (1952), 趙志強, 錢實甫.

## SIX BOARDS 六部 (吏戶禮兵刑工)
Each: 尚書 (minister) + 2 侍郎 (vice, L/R) + 4 司 (bureaus, 郎中/員外郎). Manchu-Han dual appointment 滿漢複職.
- 吏部 Personnel — appointments/evaluations, MOST powerful (factional battleground).
- 戶部 Revenue — census, tax, currency offices, national finance (Heshen's base 1780-84).
- 禮部 Rites — ceremony, exams 科舉, foreign relations UNTIL 1861.
- 兵部 War — military appointments/logistics but NOT field armies (viceroys held those).
- 刑部 Justice — one of 三法司 (w/ 大理寺 + 都察院).
- 工部 Works — construction/canals/dikes (embezzlement magnet; Heshen + Yellow River floods).
New ministries 1901/1906 (外務部 replaced Zongli Yamen 1901, ranked above the six).

## OTHER CHARACTER-OFFICE INSTITUTIONS
- 都察院 Censorate — est. 1636; 左右都御史; impeachment 彈劾 = "eyes and ears," part of 三法司, 十五道 circuits. Righteous-vs-corrupt censor archetypes; factional weapon.
- 理藩院 Lifan Yuan — est. 1636 蒙古衙門 → 1639; Inner Asia (Mongolia/Tibet/Xinjiang), appoints Ambans, Russian relations pre-1860s; staffed exclusively from 八旗; → 理藩部 1907.
- 總理衙門 Zongli Yamen — est. 1861-03-11 (Prince Gong + Wen Xiang 文祥), proto-foreign-ministry, LOW formal status, members concurrent elsewhere; oversaw 同文館 (1862); → 外務部 1901.
- 翰林院 Hanlin, 內閣 Grand Secretariat (大學士), 內務府 Imperial Household (booi 包衣, upper-3-banner bondservants; Canton Hoppo 粵海關監督 customs → imperial purse).
- Provincial: 總督 viceroy (8 regional + river viceroys; Zhili most powerful, Liangjiang richest), 巡撫 governor, 駐藏大臣 Amban, 伊犁將軍, 提督 Green Standard cmdr, banner garrison 將軍. Post-Taiping Han viceroys (Zeng/Li/Zuo) w/ personal armies eclipse the center.

## OFFICE ACQUISITION / CORRUPTION
- 科舉 exams: 生員/秀才 → 舉人 → 進士 (26,747 jinshi over 112 exams). Banner quotas (translation exams 1651; 4:2:4 Manchu/Mongol/Han). Abolished 1905-09-02 (Yuan Shikai, Zhang Zhidong).
- 捐納 sale of offices: treasury now, competence crisis + corruption.
- Factions: Heshen clique 和珅集團 (1776-99, controlled Revenue/Personnel/Council); 清流黨 "Pure Current" (1880s-1910s, Confucian statecraft 經世致用, hawkish anti-foreign, Zhang Zhidong/Chen Baochen/Zhang Peilun/Weng Tonghe 翁同龢; "罵洋務為清流,辦洋務為濁流"). Donglin 東林黨 precedent.

## SELF-STRENGTHENING 自強運動/洋務運動 (1861-1895)
- Ti-yong 中學為體西學為用 (Feng Guifen 馮桂芬); "師夷之長技以制夷" (Wei Yuan 魏源); 西學中源說.
- Sponsors: Prince Gong (Zongli Yamen/Tongwen Guan), Zeng Guofan 曾國藩 (Xiang Army 湘軍, Anqing/Jiangnan), Li Hongzhang 李鴻章 (Huai Army 淮軍, Beiyang faction, ~90% of 2nd-phase projects), Zuo Zongtang 左宗棠 (Fuzhou 福州船政局, Xinjiang 1878, 塞防 vs Li's 海防), Zhang Zhidong 張之洞 (Hanyang 漢陽鐵廠, 自強學堂→Wuhan U), Shen Baozhen 沈葆楨, Sheng Xuanhuai 盛宣懷 (官督商辦).
- Projects (industrialization beats): 江南製造總局 1865 (largest E.Asia arsenal + 翻譯館), 福州船政局 1866 (Giquel/d'Aiguebelle, naval academy ~630 cadets), 北洋水師 (Beiyang Fleet, peak 1890 78 ships/83,900t, Dingyuan 定遠/Zhenyuan 鎮遠, bases Weihaiwei/Port Arthur 旅順), 輪船招商局 1872, 開平礦務局, 上海機器織布局, 電報總局, 同文館 1862, 留美幼童 1872-81 (120 boys, recalled), railways.
- FAILURE modes (the risks a good run must beat): conservative opposition (Wo Ren 倭仁 blocks Tongwen Guan science 1867; Liu Xihong 劉錫鴻 anti-rail feng-shui), Cixi diverts ~$12M naval → Summer Palace 頤和園 1889-94 (Weng Tonghe cuts navy funding), NO central coordination (南北洋 refuse to aid Fujian fleet 1884), financial exhaustion (indemnities), 官督商辦 stifles merchants, defeats: Sino-French 1884-85 (甲申易樞 purges Prince Gong) & Sino-Japanese 1894-95 (Yalu 黃海海戰, Weihaiwei annihilation, 馬關條約 230M taels + Taiwan). Li: "紙糊的老虎."
- Secondary: Wright *Last Stand of Chinese Conservatism* (1957), Pong *Shen Pao-chen* (1994), Rawlinson (1967).

## DIPLOMATIC / FOREIGN-REACTION LAYER
- "Help for favours": foreign advisors — Robert Hart 赫德 & Imperial Maritime Customs 海關/總稅務司 (IG 1863-1908, 20-30% of central revenue, loan security, 700+ foreign staff, dual customs+diplomatic-advisor role; Van de Ven 2014, Bruner/Fairbank/Smith 1986); Ever-Victorious Army 常勝軍 (Ward 華爾, Gordon 戈登; Spence *To Change China* 1969); Beiyang naval advisor William Lang 琅威理 (dismissed 1890 flag incident → poor 1894 readiness); arsenal experts (Giquel).
- What powers wanted: MFN 利益均霑, extraterritoriality, 5% tariff cap, indemnities, spheres of influence (post-1895: Russia Manchuria, Germany Shandong, Britain Yangzi, France Yunnan/Guangxi, Japan Fujian), missionary access, diplomatic parity (legations 1861, audience/kowtow question 覲見/叩頭 resolved 1873). Burlingame Mission 蒲安臣使團 1868 (first Qing mission West; Burlingame Treaty; over-promised). Loans: Foochow 1874 → Anglo-German 1896 (customs-secured) → 1898 railway "scramble" → Rights Recovery Movement 收回利權運動.
- MEIJI benchmark 明治維新: oyatoi gaikokujin お雇い外国人 (3000+, fixed contracts, planned succession, taught replacements), Iwakura Mission 岩倉使節団 1871-73, 富国強兵/文明開化, Fukuzawa 脫亞論 1885. WHY Japan won: centralized direction vs Qing regional viceroys; wholesale institutional change vs 體用 half-measures; offensive ambition. Post-1895 Japan = model+rival: 10,000+ Chinese students to Japan 1896-1911, Japanese advisors in 新政 1901-11, 1905 憲政考察團 (5-minister constitutional mission, Meiji constitution 1889 as template). Sources: Fairbank/Reischauer/Craig (1965), Jansen (1992), Reynolds *China 1898-1912* (1993), Paine *Sino-Japanese War* (2003), Jones *Live Machines* (1980).
- Subject spillover (embrace/resist/independent-reform): Korea 朝鮮 (Ganghwa 1876, Imo 壬午軍亂 1882, Gapsin 甲申政變 1884, Yuan Shikai residency, Tientsin 1885, 1894-95 loss), Vietnam 越南 (Sino-French War → renounce 1885), Ryukyu 琉球處分 1879 (annexed, Qing inert), Burma 緬甸 (Britain 1885, decennial-tribute face-saver 1886), Siam (independent-reform escape, King Chulalongkorn), Xinjiang (Zuo reconquest, Ili/St.Petersburg 1881), Tibet 西藏 (Younghusband 1903-04, Anglo-Chinese Convention 1906).
