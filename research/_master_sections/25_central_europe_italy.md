## OWNERSHIP (political control, Feb 1763)

### AUSTRIA (Habsburg Hereditary Lands)

**Extent**:
- **Austria proper**: Archduchy of Austria, Styria, Carinthia, Carniola, Tyrol, Vorarlberg [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]
- **Bohemian Crown**: Bohemia (kingdom), Moravia, **Austrian Silesia** (small sliver; rest LOST to Prussia 1742-1763) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao, Clark]
- **Hungary**: Kingdom of Hungary, Croatia (separate kingdom in personal union), Transylvania [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]
- **Austrian Netherlands**: Belgium (Brussels, Antwerp, Liège until 1795) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]
- **Italy**: Milan/Lombardy (east of Ticino River), Tuscany (via Habsburg-Lorraine cadet from 1737) [MONOGRAPH: Carpanetto]

**Subjects**: Hungary, Croatia, Transylvania (personal union under Habsburg crown); Tuscany (Habsburg-Lorraine Grand Duke shares dynasty but autonomous); Milan (governed by Austrian viceroy, Count Firmian 1759-1782) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]

**ANACHRONISM FLAGS**:
- **Silesia LOST**: Conquered by Prussia 1740-1745, confirmed Treaty of Hubertusburg 15 Feb 1763 (day before our date). Only a tiny Austrian Silesia sliver remains. [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **Galicia NOT YET Austrian**: First Partition of Poland (1772) gives Galicia to Austria. In 1763, this territory is Polish. [MONOGRAPH: Lukowski & Zawadzki]
- **Bukovina NOT YET Austrian**: Acquired from Ottomans 1775. [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]

**Capital**: Vienna  
**Ruler**: **Maria Theresa** (r. 1740-1780), Archduchess of Austria, Queen of Hungary & Bohemia; **Francis I Stephen of Lorraine** (husband, Holy Roman Emperor 1745-1765, titular only; real power with Maria Theresa) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]

### PRUSSIA (Brandenburg-Prussia)

**Extent**:
- **Brandenburg** (Electorate): Berlin, Potsdam, Kottbus, Neumark [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **Prussia** (Kingdom, outside HRE): East Prussia (Königsberg), West Prussia PARTLY (Ermland, not Danzig/Pomerelia — that's Polish until 1772) [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **Pomerania**: Swedish Pomerania MOSTLY (acquired 1720), Hither & Further Pomerania [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **Silesia**: Lower & Upper Silesia (conquered 1740-1745, confirmed Hubertusburg 1763) [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **Magdeburg, Halberstadt, Minden, Cleves, Mark**: Westphalian/Rhenish possessions [MONOGRAPH: Clark]
- **East Frisia**: Acquired 1744 [MONOGRAPH: Clark]

**ANACHRONISM FLAGS**:
- **Posen/Poznań NOT YET Prussian**: That's Second Partition of Poland (1793). In 1763, it's Polish. [MONOGRAPH: Lukowski & Zawadzki]
- **West Prussia (Danzig corridor) NOT YET Prussian**: First Partition (1772) gives this to Prussia. [MONOGRAPH: Clark]

**Capital**: Berlin

### MAJOR FREE CITIES (Imperial Free Cities)

Approximately **51 free imperial cities** exist in 1763, autonomous republics under nominal imperial authority [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]. **By 1815, only 4 survive** (Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck, Frankfurt); **45 mediatized 1803-1806** [MONOGRAPH: Wilson].

#### Largest Free Cities (1763):

1. **HAMBURG** — Major port, ~100,000 population; survives to 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
2. **FRANKFURT AM MAIN** — Financial center, site of imperial coronations; survives to 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
3. **LÜBECK** — Hanseatic city, Baltic trade; survives to 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
4. **BREMEN** — Hanseatic port; survives to 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
5. **NÜRNBERG** (Nuremberg) — Cultural center; to Bavaria 1806 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
6. **AUGSBURG** — Mixed Catholic-Protestant (*Paritätische Reichsstadt*), banking, textiles; to Bavaria 1806 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
7. **COLOGNE** (city, distinct from Electorate) — Major Rhineland trading city; annexed by France 1794, to Prussia 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
8. **REGENSBURG** — Seat of perpetual Reichstag; to Bavaria 1810 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
9. **ULM**, **AACHEN**, **WETZLAR** (seat of Imperial Chamber Court), **ROTHENBURG**, **SPEYER**, **WORMS**, and ~40 others mediatized 1803-1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**TIER 3 (4-8 mid-tier principalities)**: Baden, Mecklenburg, Brunswick, Anhalt, Nassau, Ansbach, Bayreuth, Oldenburg [DERIVED]

**TIER 4 (ABSTRACT INTO NEIGHBORS)**:
- **Hundreds of Imperial Knights' territories** (*Reichsritterschaft*) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

## ITALY (fragmented — ~10 polities)

### SARDINIA-PIEDMONT-SAVOY (Kingdom of Sardinia)

**Extent**: Piedmont, Savoy (trans-Alpine), Nice, Sardinia (island), **Western Lombardy to Ticino River** (acquired Treaty of Worms 1743) [MONOGRAPH: Storrs]

**Capital**: Turin  
**Ruler**: **King Charles Emmanuel III** (r. 1730-1773), House of Savoy [MONOGRAPH: Storrs]

---

### GENOA (Republic)

**Extent**: Ligurian coast (Genoa, Savona, La Spezia), **CORSICA** (nominal sovereignty only — island in revolt under Pasquale Paoli from 1755; Genoese control limited to coastal fortresses) [MONOGRAPH: Costantini, Thrasher]

**Capital**: Genoa  
**Government**: Oligarchic republic (biennial doge, Great Council dominated by *alberghi* noble clans) [MONOGRAPH: Costantini]  
**Ruler**: **Doge Rodolfo Giuseppe Brignole Sale** (r. 1762-1764) in office 16 Feb 1763 [MONOGRAPH: Bitossi]

**ANACHRONISM FLAG**: **Corsica ceded to France 15 May 1768** (Treaty of Versailles); Paoli defeated 1769. In 1763, Corsica is nominally Genoese but in revolt. [MONOGRAPH: Thrasher]

**1815 Fate**: Annexed by Napoleon 1805 (Ligurian Republic puppet 1797-1805); Congress of Vienna awards Genoa to Kingdom of Sardinia 1815. **Republic extinguished.** [MONOGRAPH: Costantini]

**Extent**:

### Central Europe (pre-industrial, craft production, mercantilist trade)

- **Saxony**: Silver mining (Erzgebirge — Freiberg, Annaberg), **Meissen porcelain** (first European hard-paste, 1710), linen textiles, **Leipzig fairs** [MONOGRAPH: Ogilvie, Ingrao]

- **Austria**: Vienna (imperial capital, court consumption); wine (Niederösterreich); Styrian iron (Erzberg) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao]
- **Hungary**: Grain (Great Hungarian Plain — wheat), cattle ranching (driven to Vienna/Germany), **Tokaj wine** (premium sweet wine), copper/gold/silver (Carpathians) [MONOGRAPH: Ingrao, Kann]

## 1763-1815 TRANSFORMATION SUMMARY

**THIS IS THE SINGLE LARGEST EUROPEAN MAP CHANGE** between 1763 and 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson].

### A. Napoleonic Dissolution of HRE (1803-1806)

**1803: Reichsdeputationshauptschluss** (Imperial Recess / Final Recess) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

Context: French Revolutionary Wars (1792-1802) saw France annex left bank of Rhine (Treaty of Lunéville 1801). HRE princes lost Rhine territories; compensation = secularization + mediatization [MONOGRAPH: Wilson].

**SECULARIZATION** (*Säkularisation*) — ALL ecclesiastical territories dissolved:
- **3 ecclesiastical Electorates** (Mainz, Trier, Cologne) abolished [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **ALL prince-bishoprics** secularized (Salzburg, Würzburg, Bamberg, Münster, Paderborn, Hildesheim, Freising, Passau, Augsburg, etc.) absorbed by secular neighbors [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **All prince-abbacies** dissolved [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**MEDIATIZATION** (*Mediatisierung*) — **45 of 51 imperial free cities** lost independence:
- Absorbed by larger states: Nürnberg, Augsburg, Regensburg, Ulm, Aachen, Cologne, Worms, Speyer, etc. → Bavaria, Württemberg, Baden, Prussia [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- Only **4 survived to 1806**: Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck, Frankfurt (all survived as sovereign cities into 19th century) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**Beneficiaries**:
- **Baden** — gained Konstanz, Basel territories; raised to Electorate 1803 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **Württemberg** — gained bishoprics, abbeys, free cities; raised to Electorate 1803 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **Bavaria** — gained Würzburg, Bamberg, Freising, Augsburg, Salzburg territories, Swabia; raised to Electorate [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **Prussia** — gained Hildesheim, Paderborn, Münster, Erfurt [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**Result**: HRE shrank from **~300 states to ~100** by 1803 [MONOGRAPH: Wilson].

**1806: Dissolution of Holy Roman Empire** [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

- **Confederation of the Rhine** (*Rheinbund*) formed 12 July 1806 under French protection: Bavaria, Württemberg, Baden, Hesse-Darmstadt, Nassau, Berg, others left HRE [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **Francis II** abdicated as Holy Roman Emperor **6 August 1806** (retaining title Emperor of Austria, adopted 1804) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **HRE ceased to exist** [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**1815: German Confederation** [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

Congress of Vienna (1814-1815) created **German Confederation** (*Deutscher Bund*): **39 sovereign states** (vs. ~300 in 1763):
- Austria presided (until 1866) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **4 Free Cities** remained: Frankfurt, Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- **NO ecclesiastical states** [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]
- Prussia gained Rhineland, Westphalia [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

### B. Napoleonic Transformation of Italy (1797-1815)

- **Venice**: Extinguished 1797 (Campo Formio) → Austrian **Kingdom of Lombardy-Venetia** 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Lane]
- **Genoa**: Annexed 1805 (Ligurian Republic 1797-1805) → Kingdom of Sardinia 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Costantini]
- **Papal States**: Rome annexed 1809-1814 → restored 1815 minus Avignon [MONOGRAPH: Gross]
- **Naples + Sicily**: Murat interregnum 1808-1815 → Bourbon restored, merged into **Two Sicilies** 1816 [MONOGRAPH: Acton]
- **Tuscany**: French interregnum 1799-1814 → Habsburg-Lorraine restored 1815 [MONOGRAPH: Diaz]
- **Parma**: To Marie Louise (Napoleon's wife) 1815; reverts to Bourbon 1847 [MONOGRAPH: Carpanetto]
- **Lucca**: Made principality (Bourbon-Parma); to Tuscany 1847 [MONOGRAPH: 1763_WORLD_Italy.md]

## SUMMARY

**Central Europe (HRE)**: ~300 polities in 1763 (8 Electors, ~50 prince-bishoprics, ~51 free cities, hundreds of minor states) → 39 German Confederation states by 1815 (all ecclesiastical states dissolved 1803, free cities mostly mediatized) [MONOGRAPH: Wilson]

**Italy**: ~10 independent polities in 1763 (Venice, Genoa, Sardinia, Milan/Austrian, Tuscany, Modena, Parma, Lucca, Papal States, Naples, Sicily) → Venice/Genoa extinguished, Naples+Sicily merged, Austria gains Lombardy-Venetia, Sardinia gains Genoa [MONOGRAPH: Lane, Carpanetto, Acton]

**Economic character**: Pre-industrial 1763 (craft guilds, agriculture-dominant, mercantilist trade, luxury crafts); early industrial stirrings by 1815 (British textile competition, steam power emerging, Continental System disruption 1806-1814) [MONOGRAPH: Ogilvie, Lane]
