## OWNERSHIP (political control, Feb 1763)

### Qing periphery (neighbor context only — see 00_china.md for China proper)
- Qing Inner-Asian dominions: Manchuria, Inner+Outer Mongolia, Xinjiang (Dzungaria+Tarim just conquered 1755–59), Tibet (amban protectorate), Qinghai.
- Qing tributaries (autonomous, NOT Qing territory): Korea (Joseon, since 1637), Vietnam ("Annam", tribute via Trịnh), Ryukyu (dual Qing+Satsuma), Siam, Burma (formalized after 1769 war), Nepal, Lao states, Sulu.
- Source: WORLD_EAsia_SEAsia [MONOGRAPH]

### South Asia
- **Mughal Empire** — nominal only. Shah Alam II (r.1759–1806) is a wandering pensioner at Patna/Bihar under Awadh "protection"; direct territory ≈ Delhi environs (contested). Symbolic legitimacy still sought by successor states. Sarkar *Fall of the Mughal Empire* [MONOGRAPH]. FLAG: Mughals are NOT a territorial power.
- **Maratha Confederacy** — the real pan-Indian power, but in post-Panipat (14 Jan 1761 catastrophic defeat) recovery under young Peshwa Madhavrao I (r.1761–72). Loose confederacy: Peshwa at Pune + Chhatrapati (powerless, Satara) + great houses (Scindia/Gwalior, Holkar/Indore, Gaekwad/Baroda, Bhonsle/Nagpur). Deccan/Maharashtra heartland stable; northern axis (Delhi/Punjab) retreated post-Panipat. Gordon *The Marathas* [MONOGRAPH].
- **Bengal** — EIC-dominated post-Plassey (1757); Nawab (Mir Qasim in 1763) is a figurehead, EIC controls revenues de facto (formal diwani 1765). Capital Murshidabad; Calcutta = EIC HQ. Marshall [MONOGRAPH]. FLAG: pre-Buxar (Oct 1764) — Nawab still nominally sovereign, EIC not yet formal territorial ruler.
- **Durrani Empire (Afghanistan)** — Ahmad Shah Durrani (r.1747–72); controls Afghanistan + (weakening) Punjab/Multan/Kashmir 1761–63, withdrawing post-Panipat as Sikh misls rise.
- **Hyderabad (Deccan)** — Nizam Asaf Jah II (r.1762–1803); independent successor state, balances Marathas vs Europeans.
- **Mysore** — Hyder Ali de facto ruler from 1761 (Wodeyar puppet); rising military power, not yet seen as a major threat.
- **Awadh (Oudh)** — Nawab Shuja-ud-Daula; Faizabad/Lucknow/Allahabad; protector of Shah Alam II.
- **Rajput states** — fragmented: Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur/Mewar, etc.
- **Sikhs (Punjab)** — 12 misls forming (Jassa Singh Ahluwalia rising); NO unified state. FLAG: Sikh Empire NOT YET (Ranjit Singh 1799–1801).
- **Travancore (Kerala)** — Dharma Raja (Karthika Thirunal); modernizing, annexing small Kerala kingdoms (Cochin/Calicut pressured). Fragmented Malabar + Dutch/French/EIC enclaves.
- European coastal enclaves only: EIC (Bengal, Madras, Bombay), Dutch (Ceylon coast, Malabar), French (Pondicherry restored 1765, Mahé), Portuguese (Goa, Diu, Daman).

### Southeast Asia — Mainland
- **Konbaung Burma** — Hsinbyushin (acceded Jan 1763), aggressive expansionist; cap. Shwebo→Ava(1765). Will sack Ayutthaya 1767 and repel Qing 1765–69. Lieberman [MONOGRAPH].
- **Ayutthaya Siam** — King Ekkathat (Ban Phlu Luang dynasty), last king; cap. Ayutthaya (~150k). FLAG: still standing in 1763 — FALLS to Burma 1767 (not Bangkok/Chakri yet). Suzerain over Lanna, Lao kingdoms, N. Malay sultanates.
- **Vietnam — DIVIDED**: Lê emperor (Lê Hiển Tông) nominal at Thăng Long/Hanoi; **Trịnh lords** (Trịnh Sâm) rule the north (Đàng Ngoài, Red River Delta); **Nguyễn lords** (Nguyễn Phúc Khoát) rule the south (Đàng Trong, cap. Phú Xuân/Huế, Mekong frontier). FLAG: NOT unified — Nguyễn dynasty unification is 1802 (post-Tây Sơn).
- **Cambodia** — King Outey II; weakened, squeezed between Siam and Nguyễn Vietnam; cap. Udong.
- **Lao kingdoms** (post-1707 Lan Xang split): Luang Prabang (Burmese-leaning), Vientiane (Siamese tributary), Champasak (Siamese tributary).

### Southeast Asia — Maritime
- **Dutch VOC** — dominant commercial power, HQ Batavia (~150k). Direct: Java north coast (pasisir), Moluccas (clove/nutmeg monopoly, extirpatie), Malacca, coastal Ceylon (cinnamon), Makassar. FLAG: corporate rule, not yet Dutch-state colony (VOC dissolved 1799).
- **Java sultanates** (post-1755 Treaty of Giyanti split): Surakarta (Pakubuwono III, VOC-backed) + Yogyakarta (Hamengkubuwono I). Java ~10M — largest SEA population center.
- **Sultanates**: Aceh (Badruddin, declining), Johor-Riau (Bugis-controlled), Siak, Mataram-successors, Brunei (Muhammad Tajuddin, past peak), Banjarmasin, Sulu. Malay peninsula: Perak/Kedah/Kelantan (tin; Kedah under Siamese suzerainty).
- **Spanish Philippines** — **Manila under BRITISH occupation (Oct 1762)**; Treaty of Paris returns it to Spain, actual handover 1764. Acting Spanish gov. Simón de Anda from Bulacan. Cap-Gen under Viceroyalty of New Spain; Manila-Acapulco galleon. Pop ~1.5M. FLAG: Manila returns to Spain 1764.

### East Asia (non-China)
- **Tokugawa Japan** — sakoku isolation at peak; Shogun Tokugawa Ieharu (minor, r.1760–86, Tanuma era approaching), Empress Go-Sakuramachi (ceremonial, Kyoto). ~260 daimyō domains; foreign contact only via Nagasaki (Dutch Dejima + Chinese), Tsushima (Korea), Satsuma (Ryukyu), Matsumae (Ainu). Edo ~1M (largest city on earth). Totman/Jansen [MONOGRAPH]. FLAG: fully isolated, no Western penetration; Perry is 90 years away (1853).
- **Joseon Korea** — King Yeongjo (r.1724–76, Tangpyeong reforms; Sado incident 1762); Qing tributary since 1637 ("Little China" ideology); cap. Hanseong/Seoul (~200k). Haboush [MONOGRAPH].

### Central Asia
- **Uzbek khanates**: Bukhara (Manghit emirate rising), Khiva, Kokand.
- **Kazakh three zhüz**: Junior (Russian-leaning), Middle, Senior (Qing-leaning) — steppe confederations, culture zone.
- **Dzungaria** — DESTROYED by the Qing 1755–57 (Dzungar genocide); depopulated, absorbed into Xinjiang. FLAG: no Dzungar Khanate in 1763.

### Pacific / Oceania (essentially all unowned/indigenous — no European-sense states)
- **Australia** — CULTURE-ONLY, no tags: pre-contact Aboriginal peoples (Pama-Nyungan ~90% + non-Pama-Nyungan north); bands/clans, no state sovereignty. FLAG: no British until 1788 (First Fleet). Mulvaney & Kamminga [MONOGRAPH].
- **New Guinea** — CULTURE-ONLY: highland horticulturalists + Austronesian coastal; no state formation.
- **New Zealand** — Māori iwi (tag-worthy: rangatira, defined rohe, pā): Ngāti Tūwharetoa, Ngāpuhi, Waikato, etc. Pre-contact (Cook 1769). Ballara [MONOGRAPH].
- **Pacific islands**: Kingdom of Tonga (Tu'i Tonga, centralized hereditary monarchy — tag-worthy); Tahiti/Society Is. chiefdoms (Pomare dynasty rising); Hawaii (pre-unification chiefdoms — Kamehameha unifies ~1795); Fiji, Samoa, Marquesas = chiefdoms, mostly culture-only. Campbell/Oliver [MONOGRAPH].

## POPULATION (anchors; mod scale 4000 people = 1 unit)

### South Asia
- Total ~150–200M (Indian subcontinent). Densest: Gangetic plain (Bengal/Bihar/Awadh), Deccan, Tamil south, Kerala coast. [ESTIMATE]
- Bengal: richest province (Mughal revenue jewel), dense rice/jute delta. Maratha Deccan: Marathi Hindu core. Composition: overwhelmingly Hindu; Muslim ruling elites + NW/Bengal/Kashmir Muslim populations; Sikh Punjab; Jain/Parsi mercantile pockets; tribal (adivasi) uplands. Religion: hindu (majority), sunni/muslim (rulers + NW), sikh (Punjab misls).
- Cultures: hindustani/bengali/marathi/tamil/telugu/kannada/punjabi/rajput/gujarati etc. as mod granularity allows.

### Southeast Asia
- Java ~10M (largest SEA center). Vietnam: Trịnh north (Red River Delta) ~7M, Nguyễn south (Mekong frontier) ~2.5M. Burma (Irrawaddy), Siam (Chao Phraya, Ayutthaya ~150k city). Philippines ~1.5M. [ESTIMATE/MONOGRAPH]
- Composition: Theravada Buddhist (Burma/Siam/Cambodia/Laos), Mahayana/Confucian (Vietnam), Muslim (Malay/Sumatra/Java coast/Sulu), Catholic (Spanish Philippines lowlands), Hindu-Buddhist syncretic + animist (interior). Chinese merchant diaspora (Hoa/Sangley/Teochew) in all trade ports.

### East Asia (non-China)
- **Japan ~30M** (kokudaka ~30M koku); Japanese/Shinto-Buddhist; Edo ~1M, Osaka/Kyoto large. [MONOGRAPH: Totman]
- **Korea ~14M** (WORLD_EAsia gives ~14M; note some sources ~7M — prefer the sourced ~14M); Korean/Confucian-Buddhist; Hanseong ~200k. [MONOGRAPH: Yeongjo Sillok-based]

### Central Asia
- Sparse: Uzbek khanate oases (Bukhara/Samarkand/Khiva/Kokand — Turkic/Tajik, Sunni Muslim), Kazakh steppe (nomadic Turkic, Sunni/Tengri syncretic). Xinjiang depopulated (Dzungar genocide). [ESTIMATE]

### Pacific / Oceania
- Australia Aboriginal ~750k–1M (pre-contact estimates, wide range); NZ Māori ~100k; Pacific islands small (Tonga/Tahiti/Hawaii tens of thousands each). All indigenous religions/cultures. [ESTIMATE]

- Mughals nominal (pensioner), NOT a territorial power; Marathas are the real pan-Indian power (post-Panipat 1761 recovery).
- Bengal = EIC-dominated but Nawab nominally sovereign (pre-Buxar 1764); no British Raj; European holdings = coastal enclaves only.
- Sikh Empire NOT YET — only 12 misls forming (Ranjit Singh 1799).
- Ayutthaya Siam STILL STANDING (falls to Burma 1767); NOT Bangkok/Chakri/Thonburi yet.
- Vietnam DIVIDED (Trịnh north / Nguyễn south / Lê puppet); NOT unified Nguyễn (1802).
- Konbaung Burma ascendant (Hsinbyushin acceded Jan 1763); Ava capital from 1765.
- Manila under British occupation Feb 1763 → returns to Spain 1764; Philippines = Spanish (Cap-Gen under New Spain).
- Java divided Surakarta/Yogyakarta (post-1755 Giyanti); VOC = corporate rule (dissolved 1799, no Dutch-state colony yet); no British Singapore/Raffles.
- Japan = isolated Tokugawa (sakoku peak); Korea = Qing tributary (Yeongjo).
- Dzungaria destroyed/depopulated by Qing 1755–57 — no Dzungar Khanate.
- Nepal = pre-1768 (Gorkha conquest of Kathmandu 1768–69) — Malla city-states + rising Gorkha.
- Australia/New Guinea = culture-only (no British till 1788); NZ Māori iwi + Pacific chiefdoms (Tonga/Tahiti/Hawaii) pre-contact (Cook 1769); Hawaii pre-unification (Kamehameha ~1795).
