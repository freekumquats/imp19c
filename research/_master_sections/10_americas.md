## OWNERSHIP (political control, Feb 1763)

### Spain — Viceroyalty of New Spain
- Extent: core Mexico; Captaincy-General of Guatemala (Chiapas→Costa Rica); northern frontier (Nueva Vizcaya, Nuevo León, Nuevo Santander, Texas, Nuevo México); Caribbean (Cuba, Santo Domingo/E. Hispaniola, Puerto Rico); the Philippines (via Manila galleon). NEW 1763: Louisiana west of the Mississippi + New Orleans (from France; formal handover 1769).
- Capital: Mexico City. Key cities: Puebla, Guanajuato, Zacatecas, Veracruz, Havana, Guatemala (Antigua).
- Subject/overlord: viceroyalty of the Spanish Crown; Guatemala + Cuba subordinate captaincies-general.
- Source: Kuethe & Andrien 2014; WORLD_Americas §II [MONOGRAPH]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: Alta California NOT colonized (first mission San Diego 1769); no independent Mexico/Central America (1821); Louisiana admin still transitional (French locals in place).

### Spain — Viceroyalty of Peru
- Extent: core Peru (Audiencias of Lima, Cuzco); Upper Peru / Audiencia of Charcas (modern Bolivia, incl. Potosí); Captaincy-General of Chile; **Río de la Plata region (modern Argentina, Paraguay, Uruguay) governed from Lima**; Audiencia of Quito (Ecuador, under Peru 1751–1802).
- Capital: Lima. Key cities: Cuzco, Potosí, Charcas/Sucre, Arequipa, Buenos Aires (subordinate), Asunción, Quito.
- Source: Fisher *Bourbon Peru* 2003; WORLD_Americas §II.A [MONOGRAPH]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: **Viceroyalty of Río de la Plata does NOT exist (created 1776)** — Argentina/Paraguay/Uruguay are under Peru/Lima in 1763.

### Spain — Viceroyalty of New Granada
- Extent: core Nueva Granada (modern Colombia, Audiencia of Bogotá); Captaincy-General of Venezuela (loosely organized under Bogotá — the unified C-G is 1777); Panama (Audiencia of Panamá). (Quito is under Peru in 1763, not New Granada.)
- Capital: Santafé de Bogotá. Key cities: Cartagena, Panamá, Caracas, Maracaibo, Popayán.
- Source: Kuethe & Andrien 2014; WORLD_Americas §II.A.3 [MONOGRAPH]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: no independent Gran Colombia/Colombia/Venezuela/Ecuador; CRT independent Cartagena republic (~1811) is anachronistic — must be New Granada (SFB).

### Portugal — State of Brazil (+ State of Maranhão e Grão-Pará)
- Extent: Atlantic Brazil. Northeast sugar (Bahia/Salvador, Pernambuco/Recife); center (Minas Gerais gold, Rio, São Paulo); north (Pará/Maranhão, separate administrative state until 1774, cap. Belém); contested southern frontier (Santa Catarina, Rio Grande, Colônia do Sacramento on the Plata).
- Capital: **Rio de Janeiro (moved from Salvador in 1763 — the exact start year)**. Key cities: Salvador, Recife, Vila Rica/Ouro Preto, São Paulo, Belém.
- Subject/overlord: colony of the Portuguese Crown under a Viceroy (Conde da Cunha, 1763–67).
- Source: Maxwell *Pombal* 1995; Boxer; WORLD_Americas §III [MONOGRAPH]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: no independent Brazil (1822); Jesuit missions dissolved (Jesuits expelled 1759); gold past peak; interior Amazon is Indigenous, not Portuguese-controlled.

### Britain — Thirteen Colonies
- Extent: Atlantic seaboard — New England (Mass. incl. Maine, NH, RI, CT), Middle (NY, NJ, PA, DE), South (MD, VA, NC, SC, GA). Settlement barred west of the Appalachians by the Royal Proclamation of 1763.
- Capital/key cities: (no capital) Boston, New York, Philadelphia, Charleston, Baltimore.
- Subject/overlord: British colonies (NO United States — independence 1776).
- Source: Anderson *Crucible of War* 2000; Royal Proclamation 1763 [PRIMARY]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: NO USA tag; NO settlement in trans-Appalachia/Ohio (Proclamation Line + Pontiac) — that interior is Native.

### Britain — Canada / Quebec + Maritimes (NEW 1763)
- Extent: Province of Quebec (St. Lawrence: Montreal, Quebec City, Trois-Rivières) ceded by France; Nova Scotia/Acadia (British since 1713); Newfoundland; Rupert's Land (HBC charter). Pays d'en Haut (Great Lakes forts) + Illinois Country east of Mississippi nominally British but Native-dominated (Pontiac's War, May 1763).
- Capital/key: Quebec City, Halifax.
- NOTE (mod handling): at the exact Feb 1763 snapshot the cession is days old; French-Canadian (canadien/quebecois) population and Catholic religion remain in place. French garrison towns Detroit, Kaskaskia, Vincennes still culturally French.
- Source: Anderson 2000; Calloway 2006 [MONOGRAPH]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: no independent/loyalist Ontario settler wave (post-1783); Detroit/Kaskaskia/St.Louis handled below.

### Britain — West Indies
- Extent: Jamaica, Barbados, Leeward Is. (Antigua, St Kitts, Nevis, Montserrat), Bahamas (British since 1718); NEW 1763 "Ceded Islands" — Dominica, St Vincent, Grenada, Tobago. British Honduras (Belize) = tolerated logwood cutters on nominally Spanish coast.
- Source: Dunn *Sugar and Slaves* 1972; Treaty of Paris [MONOGRAPH/PRIMARY]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: **Trinidad is SPANISH until 1797** (not British in 1763); **St. Lucia is FRENCH** (British only from 1814).

### France — Caribbean + Guiana (remnants)
- Extent: Saint-Domingue (W. Hispaniola, cap. Cap-Français) — wealthiest colony in the hemisphere; Guadeloupe & Martinique (returned by Britain 1763); French Guiana (cap. Cayenne); St-Pierre & Miquelon (fishing).
- Source: Burnard & Garrigus *Plantation Machine* 2016; Treaty of Paris [MONOGRAPH/PRIMARY]
- ANACHRONISM FLAGS: no independent Haiti (1804) — Saint-Domingue is French, enslaved-majority.

### Netherlands & Denmark — Caribbean/Guiana
- Dutch: Suriname (cap. Paramaribo), Curaçao/Aruba/Bonaire, St Eustatius (smuggling hub), Saba, S. St Maarten. Danish: St Thomas, St John, St Croix.
- NOTE: **Demerara/Essequibo/Berbice (Georgetown, Guyana) are DUTCH in 1763** (British only from 1796).
- Source: WORLD_Americas §VI [MONOGRAPH-derived]

### INDEPENDENT / unconquered zones (NOT European-controlled in 1763)
- **Comanche** (Numunuu) — dominant power of the southern Plains (TX/OK/NM/KS), extracting tribute from Spanish NM & Texas. Hämäläinen 2008 [MONOGRAPH].
- **Sioux/Lakota-Dakota** — expanding across northern Plains (Dakotas/MT/MN/WY). Hämäläinen 2019 [MONOGRAPH].
- **Pontiac's Confederacy & pays d'en haut** — Ottawa/Ojibwe/Potawatomi/Wyandot/Seneca/Delaware/Shawnee/Miami; open war on British forts from May 1763. Dowd 2002 [MONOGRAPH].
- **Iroquois/Haudenosaunee Six Nations** — NY/PA, weakened but a diplomatic power.
- **Osage** — effective power of the lower Missouri valley (nominal Spanish Louisiana). Osage ~6–7k.
- **Cherokee** (S. Appalachians, post-Anglo-Cherokee War 1758–61) & **Creek Confederacy** (GA/AL/FL) — autonomous.
- **Mapuche / Araucanía** — INDEPENDENT south of the Bío-Bío; War of Arauco ongoing, frontier by *parlamento*.
- **Gran Chaco peoples** (Guaycurú/Wichí) & **Patagonia** (Tehuelche) — unconquered.
- **Amazon interior** — hundreds of Indigenous nations; Portuguese presence limited to lower Amazon + missions.
- **Alaska / Russian America** — NASCENT: no permanent Russian settlement until Kodiak 1784; Russian America Co. 1799. In 1763 the coast is Indigenous (Tlingit, Alutiiq/Sugpiaq, Aleut, Yup'ik). Should NOT be a held Russian territory.

## POPULATION (anchors; scale note: mod uses 4000 people = 1 unit)

### Mexico (New Spain core)
- Magnitude: ~4–4.5M (New Spain core, excl. Central America). [ESTIMATE]
- Composition: heavily indigenous — Pacific/south ~68%, eastern/central ~75% indigenous; mestizo belt in Bajío/silver north; Spanish/criollo urban elite; small enslaved-African coastal (Veracruz/Acapulco).
- Cultures/religion: nahuatl/zapotec/tzeltal/purepecha/maya + mexican (mestizo) + castilian; Catholic (indigenous folk-Catholic syncretism).

### Central America / Guatemala
- Magnitude: ~1M. Highland Guatemala indigenous-majority (Maya). [ESTIMATE]
- Cultures/religion: quichean/maya highlands, mestizo lowlands; Catholic.

### Andes — Peru (core + Upper Peru/Bolivia)
- Magnitude: Peru core ~1.1–1.4M; Upper Peru/Bolivia ~0.6–0.8M. [ESTIMATE]
- Composition: **70–80% indigenous** (Quechua/Aymara); Spanish/criollo + mestizo urban; small African (coast/Lima). Potosí mining pop via mita.
- Cultures/religion: quechua/aymara + peruvian/castilian; Catholic + Andean inti/folk survivals.

### Quito / Ecuador
- Magnitude: ~0.5–0.6M. [ESTIMATE] Indigenous-majority highlands (Quechua); mestizo/Spanish urban. Catholic.

### New Granada / Colombia
- Magnitude: research anchor ~0.8–1.0M (on-disk currently ~1.2M → trim). [ESTIMATE]
- Composition: mestizo + indigenous (Muisca-descended highlands) + African (Cartagena/Chocó/Cauca — enslaved mining/plantation); Catholic + muisca survivals.

### Venezuela
- Magnitude: research anchor ~0.5–0.7M (on-disk ~0.85M → trim). [ESTIMATE]
- Composition: mestizo/pardo, Spanish/criollo (Caracas cacao elite), enslaved African (coastal cacao), indigenous (Guajira/interior); Catholic.

### Chile
- Magnitude: colonial (N of Bío-Bío) ~0.4–0.5M [ESTIMATE, on-disk ~0.26M → RAISE]; **independent Mapuche ~0.2–0.3M** (Araucanía).
- Composition: colonial = mestizo + Spanish + Mapuche-descended; Mapuche = independent, mapuche culture / indigenous religion.

### Río de la Plata (Argentina / Uruguay / Paraguay)
- Magnitude: **colonial ~0.3–0.4M** (Buenos Aires, Litoral, Tucumán, Asunción, Banda Oriental). [ESTIMATE]
- IMPORTANT: the unconquered Pampas/Chaco/Patagonia indigenous (Tehuelche, Guaycurú, Ranquel) are SEPARATE and legitimately large — do not fold them into the colonial anchor. Guaraní mission pop ~100–150k (Jesuits expelled 1767).
- Composition: colonial criollo/mestizo + African (Buenos Aires ~30% afro at times) + Guaraní (Paraguay/missions); Catholic.

### Brazil (by region)
- Magnitude: ~1.5–2.0M total. Bahia 300–350k, Pernambuco 250–300k, Rio 200–250k, Minas Gerais 300–350k, São Paulo 100–150k, Amazon/Maranhão-Pará 100–150k, Rio Grande 50–70k. [ESTIMATE]
- Composition: **~40% enslaved African, ~30% mixed (pardo), remainder white Portuguese + detribalized indigenous**; enslaved share highest in sugar Northeast + mining Minas. Catholic + Afro-syncretic (candomble/dahoman precursors — mod-defined, treat as folk).
- Cultures: brazilian (Luso) + afro-brazilian/African + tupi/interior indigenous.

### Caribbean sugar islands
- Saint-Domingue: enslaved-MAJORITY (~85%+); afro_haitian + French planter minority. Cap-Français.
- Jamaica ~200k (mostly enslaved by 1770s); Barbados (enslaved-majority, soil exhaustion); Cuba pre-boom (~230k, sugar developing post-1762 Havana occupation); Danish/Dutch islands enslaved-majority. Catholic (Spanish/French) / Anglican (British) / Reformed (Dutch).

### Thirteen Colonies
- Magnitude: ~1.6M total; ~400k enslaved African concentrated in the South (VA ~40% enslaved; SC majority-enslaved lowcountry). [MONOGRAPH: Anderson/estimates]
- Composition: British/anglo settler majority (New England ~all free; Middle mixed; South planter+enslaved); Anglican/Congregationalist/Presbyterian/Quaker. Enslaved = afro/black + African cultures.

### New France / Quebec
- Magnitude: **~70k European (canadien)** across all of New France (St. Lawrence core + scattered posts). Plus regional Native (Abenaki, Huron/Wendat, Mi'kmaq). [MONOGRAPH: Calloway/Anderson]
- Composition: quebecois/canadien, Catholic. NOTE: on-disk ~216k likely overcounts Europeans ~3× unless counting Native — flag for trim.

### Native nations (population anchors — Calloway 2006, Hämäläinen 2008/2019, Cook 1976, Boyd 1999, Weber 1992; all [MONOGRAPH])
Interior North America total ~950k–1.2M (~240–320 units at 4000:1). By region (units in parens):
- **Great Lakes / pays d'en haut** ~75–100k (19–25u): Ojibwe/Anishinaabe 25–35k, Odawa 8–10k, Potawatomi 4–6k, Miami 4.5–6k, Shawnee 3–4k, Ho-Chunk 3–4k, Menominee 3–4k, Illinois 2–3k (reduced from ~10k in 1680), Wyandot 1.5–2k. French posts ~3–4k (Detroit 800–2000, Kaskaskia ~1000, Michilimackinac ~500).
- **Upper Mississippi / Great Forests** ~25–30k (6–8u): Dakota/Santee Sioux 15–20k, W. Ojibwe 8–10k, Cree 2–3k.
- **Great Plains** ~150–200k (38–50u): Comanche 20–30k, Lakota/W.Sioux 15–25k, Blackfoot 15–20k, Pawnee 10–12k, Osage 6–7k, Crow 4–5k, Cheyenne 3.5–4.5k, Arapaho 3–4k, Wichita 3–4k, Mandan/Hidatsa/Arikara 3–4k each (smallpox 1781 NOT yet), Kiowa 2–3k, Omaha, Kansa, Ponca, Plains Apache 3–5k.
- **Cascadia / Pacific NW** ~150–200k (38–50u): Columbia River (Chinook/Yakama/Nez Perce) 50–70k, Coast Salish/Puget 30–40k, Interior Salish 15–20k, Oregon coast 15–20k, Sahaptin 10–12k (smallpox 1770s NOT yet — very high density). ZERO European contact.
- **Mountain West / Great Basin** ~50–70k (13–18u): Shoshone 15–20k, Ute 8–10k, N. Paiute 7–9k, S. Paiute 5–7k, W. Shoshone 3–4k, Bannock 2–3k. ZERO Europeans.
- **California** ~250–310k (63–78u): densest region north of Mesoamerica (Cook 1976, at 1769 contact so ~same in 1763). Central Coast 60–70k (Chumash/Ohlone), SF Bay 25–30k, Sacramento V. 50–60k, San Joaquin V. 40–50k (Yokuts), S. California 30–40k (Tongva/Luiseño/Kumeyaay), N. Coast 25–30k (Pomo/Yurok/Hupa), NE 15–20k (Modoc), desert/Colorado R. 5–10k (Mojave/Quechan). **ENTIRELY unconquered — Spanish missions begin 1769, six years away.**
- **American Southwest** ~60–70k (15–18u): **MIXED** — Spanish own only the Rio Grande corridor (Santa Fe/Albuquerque/El Paso, ~10–12k Spanish+genízaro+~2k mission Pueblo, 2–3u). Independent: Navajo 15–20k, W. Apache 8–10k, Tohono O'odham 6–8k, Pueblo (19) 9–11k under nominal Spanish rule, Hopi 2.5–3k (never reconquered after 1680), Jicarilla/Mescalero/Lipan Apache, Pima, Quechan, Yavapai. Weber 1992: "Apaches controlled more of the Southwest than did Spaniards."
- **Canadian West & North** ~120–180k (30–45u): Cree 30–40k (fur middlemen), Dene/Chipewyan 15–20k, Inuit 20–25k, Blackfoot (see Plains), Assiniboine 8–10k, + NW Coast (Tlingit/Haida/Tsimshian/Kwakwaka'wakw/Nuu-chah-nulth) + Interior Salish/Dakelh. HBC = ~300–500 at Hudson Bay posts only; "Rupert's Land" charter is a PAPER claim, interior posts not until 1770s (Cumberland House 1774). BC Pacific coast not even British-claimed (Nootka 1790).
- **Alaska** ~70–90k (18–23u): Inuit (Iñupiat/Yup'ik) 30–40k, Aleut 12–15k (Russian hunters devastating them post-1741), Tlingit 10–15k, Athabaskan 12–15k. Russian promyshlenniki ~200–400 SEASONAL hunters in Aleutians only; NO permanent settlement until Kodiak 1784 (Black 2004).
- Religion for Native nations: mod uses waashat (Plateau/Plains/Great Basin/Southwest) and earth_lodge (some Great Basin/California), inuit (Arctic/Alaska), NOT gaihwiio (Handsome Lake 1799 — anachronistic).

## KEY ANACHRONISM CHECKLIST (Americas)
- NO United States (British Thirteen Colonies; independence 1776). NO independent Spanish-American nations (all colonial). NO independent Brazil (1822). NO independent Haiti (French Saint-Domingue; 1804).
- Viceroyalty of Río de la Plata does NOT exist (1776) — Argentina/Paraguay/Uruguay under Peru (Lima).
- Trans-Appalachia / Ohio Country / pays d'en haut = Native-controlled (Proclamation Line + Pontiac's War), NOT settler states.
- Trinidad = Spanish (till 1797); St. Lucia = French (till 1814); Georgetown/Guyana (Demerara-Essequibo-Berbice) = Dutch (till 1796).
- St. Louis founded 1764 → in Feb 1763 it should be Osage/empty, NOT a French town.
- Alta California NOT colonized (first mission 1769); Baja California missions exist. Russian Alaska NOT held (first settlement 1784) → Indigenous.
- Detroit / Kaskaskia / Vincennes = French-Canadian culture/Catholic (garrison towns, cession days old).
- gaihwiio religion (Handsome Lake, 1799) is anachronistic → waashat. Métis ethnogenesis is 1810s → Cree/other.
- Brazil capital = Rio de Janeiro (moved 1763). Jesuits expelled (Brazil 1759, Spanish America 1767) — missions winding down.
- Florida = British (1763–1783), just taken from Spain. Cuba/Manila returned to Spain (1763). Louisiana west of Mississippi + New Orleans = Spanish (from France 1762).
- Comanche/Sioux/Osage/Mapuche/Chaco/Patagonia/Amazon interior = independent/unconquered, sized per Calloway/Hämäläinen.
