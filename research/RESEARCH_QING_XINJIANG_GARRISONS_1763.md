## 1. COMMAND GEOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE

### Ili General 伊犁將軍 (est. 1762)
- **Establishment:** Qianlong 27 (1762), following the conquest of Dzungaria (1755–57) and the Altishahr Khoja revolts (1758–59).
- **First holder:** 明瑞 Mingrui (Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner), served 1762/11–1767/3. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]
- **Seat:** Huiyuan 惠遠城, built 1763–66, completed 1766/1. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 惠遠城]
- **Jurisdiction:** Unified military-civil authority over all Xinjiang (north and south of Tianshan), reporting to Lifan Yuan 理藩院. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Xinjiang under Qing rule]

### Subordinate Command Structure
The Ili General commanded a tiered network of **參贊大臣** (Assistant Ministers), **辦事大臣** (Resident Ministers), **領隊大臣** (Regimental Commanders), and **都統** (Military Governors). [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]

## 2. NORTH vs SOUTH GARRISON MODEL

### DZUNGARIA (North): Heavy Direct Garrison + Settlement Colonization
- **Model:** Permanent Eight Banners + Green Standard garrisons, reinforced by redeployed non-Manchu banner battalions (Sibe, Solon, Chahar, Ölöt) and Han/Hui tuntian 屯田 military colonies.
- **Settlement:** The Qing settled "Manchu, Sibo, Daurs, Solons, Han Chinese, Hui Muslims, Salar, and Muslim Taranchis in the north," with Han/Hui the largest groups. State farms settled 155,000 Han from 1760–1830, ALL in Dzungaria and Ürümqi. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Xinjiang under Qing rule]
- **Key cities:** Ili valley (Huiyuan/nine cities of Ili 伊犁九城), Ürümqi (Dihua 迪化), Tarbagatai, Barkol, Hami—all had walled garrison cities (駐防城 or 滿城).

### ALTISHAHR/TARIM BASIN (South): Light Rotating Garrison over Indirect Rule
- **Model:** Small Chinese garrisons stationed at oasis garrison-cities (漢城), rotating on 3–5 year tours (換防). The bulk of governance through co-opted begs 伯克 (Turkic officials managing taxation, irrigation, justice, religious affairs under Qing Resident Ministers).
- **Settlement ban:** Permanent Han settlement PROHIBITED. Only soldiers and merchants allowed temporarily. As of 1870, "only a few Chinese merchants and soldiers in several garrisons among the Muslim population." [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Xinjiang under Qing rule]
- **Key cities:** Kashgar (political center), Yarkand, Aksu, Ush, Khotan, Kucha, Karashahr—each had a Resident Minister + small garrison + indigenous beg administration.
- **Rationale:** The Qing avoided large-scale military occupation to minimize cost and unrest in the densely-populated, Islamicate oasis cities. The 1758–59 Altishahr revolts demonstrated the volatility of direct rule. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伯克制度]

## 3. TROOP TYPES & DISTRIBUTION

### Eight Banners 八旗 (Manchu + Mongol + Han Banners)
- **Huiyuan 惠遠 Manchu garrison (滿營):** "超4,800人" (over 4,800 bannermen) during Qianlong reign, plus dependents totaling over 42,000 people in the city. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 惠遠城] **⚠ 1763 FLAG:** Huiyuan city completed 1766; the 4,800 figure may reflect post-1766 strength. Early garrisons (1762–65) were at Taleqi 塔勒奇城 and Suiding 綏定城 before Huiyuan was finished.
- **Huining 惠寧城** (second Manchu city in Ili valley, part of the nine cities of Ili): Separate banner garrison, commanded by a Regimental Commander under the Ili General. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]
- **Ürümqi Gonging 鞏寧城** (built 1772): "駐滿營官兵" (stationed Manchu banner troops). [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 烏魯木齊] **⚠ 1763 FLAG:** Gonging city built 1772, nine years after 1763 start. The 1763 Ürümqi garrison would have been at an earlier location or smaller scale.
- **Barkol Huining 會寧城** (built 1772): Banner garrison. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 哈密] **⚠ 1763 FLAG:** City built 1772.

### Redeployed Non-Manchu Banner Battalions
Ili General commanded five **領隊大臣** (Regimental Commanders) for ethnically-organized battalions settled in the Ili valley:

1. **Sibe 錫伯營 (Sibe Battalion):** 1,020 soldiers + 3,275 dependents departed in 1764 (Qianlong 29), total 5,050 Sibe reached Ili. Organized into 6 niru 牛錄 (later 8), settled on the far bank of the Ili River from Fort Huiyuan (modern Qapqal). Self-sufficient by farming; secondary status to Manchu garrisons. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Sibe people; zh.wikipedia 錫伯族]  
   **⚠ 1763 FLAG:** Sibe migration occurred IN 1764, one year AFTER the 1763 start date. In Feb 1763, the Sibe battalion had NOT YET arrived. The 1763 OOB should either (a) exclude them, or (b) note the Feb-vs-mid-year discrepancy if the game starts mid-1763.

2. **Solon 索倫營 (Solon Battalion):** Tungusic people from Manchuria, redeployed to Ili. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍; en.wikipedia Xinjiang under Qing rule mentions "Solons" settled in the north]

3. **Chahar 察哈爾營 (Chahar Battalion):** Inner Mongolian banner troops (Chahar Mongols), redeployed. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍; en.wikipedia Xinjiang under Qing rule]

4. **Ölöt 厄魯特營 (Ölöt/Oirat Battalion):** Mongolic (Oirat-descended) troops, likely drawn from Qinghai or surrendered Dzungars. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]

5. **Huining 惠寧城 Regimental Commander:** Manchu banner garrison at Huining city (second city in the Ili valley). [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]

### Green Standard Army 綠營
- **Deployment:** Predominantly in NORTH (Dzungaria): Ili valley (six of the nine cities of Ili were Green Standard 綠營 cities), Ürümqi, Tarbagatai. Also rotated to SOUTH (Tarim Basin) on 3-year tours (later extended to 5 years). [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Green Standard Army; zh.wikipedia 綠營_(清朝)]
- **Source provinces:** Shaanxi 陝西 and Gansu 甘肅 Green Standard troops rotated to Kashgaria (Tarim oasis garrisons). [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Green Standard Army]
- **Ili valley Green Standard command:** The 總兵 (Commander-in-Chief) of the Ili Green Standard was based at Suiding 綏定城, one of the six Green Standard cities in the Ili nine-cities. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 伊犁將軍]
- **Tarbagatai:** Built by 參贊大臣 Choleduo 綽勒多 leading Green Standard troops in 1764; garrison remained Green Standard. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 塔爾巴哈臺]

### Tuntian 屯田 (Military Agricultural Colonies)
- **Types:** 兵屯 (soldier-farmer colonies), 民屯 (civilian colonies), 回屯 (Hui Muslim colonies). [UNVERIFIED: zh.wikipedia 屯田 is a disambiguation page, specific Xinjiang tuntian details NOT reached]
- **Geography:** Concentrated in Dzungaria (Ili valley, Ürümqi corridor). The《新疆識略》records察哈爾營、索倫營、錫伯營 troop numbers, garrison situations, weapons, and drill training. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: zh.wikipedia 新疆識略]
- **Function:** Self-sufficient food production for garrisons + land reclamation. Manchu bannermen were state-supplied; non-Manchu battalions (Sibe, Solon, Chahar) and Han Green Standard colonies were required to farm. [ENCYCLOPEDIC: en.wikipedia Sibe people]
