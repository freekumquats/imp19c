# qing_fiscal_buildings.txt — [buildings-research 2026-07-27] the MONETARY,
# BANKING and COMMERCIAL plant of the Qing empire, distinct from the customs
# house (qing_customs_buildings.txt) and the ever-normal granary. Concrete
# on-map counterparts to the tax / trade-route / commerce layers.
#
#  • 錢局 Provincial Mint — the cash-coin foundry casting 制錢. SEEDED at the
#    capital and provincial mint cities; a tax anchor.
#  • 票號 Shanxi Draft Bank — the piaohao remittance bank that moved silver across
#    the empire by draft. SEEDED in Shanxi; strong trade-route reach.
#  • 會館 Merchant Guild Hall — the native-place association organising inter-
#    regional trade. Player-buildable; a broadly useful commerce building.
#  • 漕倉 Tribute Grain Depot — the Grand-Canal tribute-grain store, distinct from
#    the price-stabilising 常平倉. Food storage on the canal corridor.
#  • 釐金局 Likin Station — the internal-transit tax barrier (1850s+). Raises tax
#    but throttles trade and irritates the merchants: a fiscal double edge.
#  • 大清銀行 Imperial Bank — the modern central bank (1905/08). Late-game capstone
#    of monetary modernisation.
#
# Schema mirrors qing_customs_buildings.txt. Modifier VALUES on the proven scale.
