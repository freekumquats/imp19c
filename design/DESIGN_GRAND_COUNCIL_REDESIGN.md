## THE LOCKED MODEL (user, 2026-07-06)

1. **The Grand Council = exactly the office-holders. There is NO separate "grand councillor" population.** Delete the whole abstract-seat layer: `qing_council_members` list as a hand-filled thing, `qing_council_seat_cap`/`seat_count`, `QING_council_seat_character`, the `qing_gov_council_seat` verb + its character-window button + the tab's "seat" action. 2. **You join the council by being APPOINTED TO AN OFFICE.** Roster = the set of filled office cards. No seat action; only appoint-to-office.
3. **Grand Chancellor (領班軍機大臣) is ITS OWN OFFICE** — appointed like any other (new `qing_office_chancellor_holder` var + "Appoint Grand Chancellor" button), NOT a hat elevated onto an existing holder. Collapse the old chief machinery (`qing_council_chief`, `QING_council_make_chief`, "Name Chief" verb/button) into the office system. As its own office it now NEEDS a defined country modifier (user asked to "include him in the modifier table") — pending the rework or user spec.
4. **Emperor Emeritus (太上皇, event/Napoleon-only)** stays an office card shown only when occupied (already built that way); read-only, not appointable from the tab.
