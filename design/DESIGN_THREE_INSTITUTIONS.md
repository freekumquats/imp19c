**ZH RESEARCH FINDINGS (agent, 2026-07-10) — HISTORY CORRECTIONS that reshape the design:**
- 南書房 = Kangxi's (1677) inner-court literary/secretarial body in 乾清宮西南 (乾清門 WEST side). Real power center UNDER KANGXI (drafted edicts, 「非崇班貴戚、上所親信者不得入」); DEMOTED to honorary/literary after the 軍機處 was set up under Yongzheng (~late 1720s—early 1730s; don't cite an exact year, per Bartlett). **By 1763 (Qianlong) it is HIGH-PRESTIGE, LOW-POWER** — a literary-attendant corps, real policy power sits in the 軍機處. Abolished 1898. So the game model: high court-prestige/legitimacy + emperor-affinity + literary/soft influence, NOT a policy-power organ.
- **CORRECTION 1 — leadership title:** there is NO formal "Grand Preceptor of the Southern Study." Members held NO special office-title, just their original rank + the posting label 「南書房行走」 ("on duty in the Southern Study"). The 師傅/總師傅/帝師 titles belong to the 上書房 (the princes' schoolroom, 乾清門 EAST side) — a DIFFERENT institution. Recommended title = descriptive: **"Chief Literary Attendant of the Southern Study" (南書房行走之首 / 首席)**, NOT "Grand Preceptor." If the user insists on "Grand Preceptor," the historically-correct home for it is 上書房總師傅 (Grand Preceptor of the UPPER Study, tutors princes) — a separate feature.
- **CORRECTION 2 — staffing ≠ top-3 laureates:** entry was NOT gated on 狀元/榜眼/探花 (一甲 top-3). It drew from Hanlin (進士 who passed 朝考/館選), chosen for 才品 + esp. 書法 (calligraphy) + the emperor's personal trust. Modelling it as "top-3 laureates only" is a historical oversimplification. Model: draw from the jinshi/Hanlin scholar pool weighted by finesse/charisma (learning+eloquence) + affinity to the throne; the 狀元 laureate is a natural but not exclusive member.
- Named members (verified) for flavour: 張英, 高士奇 (both 1677 first cohort), 陳廷敬, 查慎行, 朱彝尊, 王士禎, 沈荃, 方苞. 1763-specific names UNCONFIRMED (沈德潛/錢陳群 are Qianlong literary favourites but their formal 南書房 posting is unverified — use as "Qianlong literary attendant" archetype, don't assert the posting).
- Cite-able sources: 《清史稿·職官志》, 《康熙起居注》, 《南書房記注》(第一歷史檔案館), 傅宗懋《清代軍機處組織及職掌之研究》(1967), Bartlett *Monarchs and Ministers* (1991), Silas Wu *Communication and Imperial Control* (1970).
**EN RESEARCH FINDINGS (agent, 2026-07-10) — CORROBORATES the ZH agent on every key point:**
- Confirms: 南書房 (1677, Kangxi) = emperor's PRIVATE inner-court study (SW of 乾清宮, by 月華門); real informal power under Kangxi; demoted to prestige/literary after the 軍機處 (~1729-1732); by 1763 = high-prestige literary-companion posting, real power in the Grand Council. Abolished 1898.
- Confirms NO "Grand Preceptor" and NO formal head at all: everyone served as 「南書房行走」 (xíngzǒu = "detailed to attend", an ATTACHMENT not an office); a member kept his substantive Hanlin/cabinet title. Senior member = whoever held the highest concurrent office + most imperial favour (de-facto "first among equals", NOT a titled post). No 南書房 equivalent of the Hanlin's 掌院學士.
- Confirms 師傅/太傅/太師 belong to the TUTORING system: the 上書房 (Upper Study / princes' schoolroom, 乾清門 EAST side, inside 乾清門) has a real **上書房總師傅 (Chief Tutor of the Upper Schoolroom)** + 師傅 tutors + Manchu/Mongol riding-masters 諳達 — teaches PRINCES, no political role. This is the true home of a "Grand Preceptor/Grand Tutor" title.
- Confirms staffing ≠ top-3 laureates: drew from Hanlin (elite jinshi), selected by the emperor's PERSONAL judgment on scholarship + calligraphy + trust ("才品兼優"). Top laureates over-represented but NOT a rule. WILDCARD precedent: 高士奇 was NEVER a jinshi — rose purely on calligraphy/talent → supports an optional "talent wildcard" recruit path.
- Model it as: an ATTACHMENT/honor a Hanlin/Grand-Secretary character holds (南書房行走), benefits = prestige + imperial-favour/proximity + cultural-project output + a PROMOTION PIPELINE into the Grand Council — NOT raw policy power.
- **READY-MADE 1763 CHARACTER: 梁詩正 Liang Shizheng** — 探花 (1730, 3rd-place laureate), 南書房行走 from Qianlong's accession, master calligrapher/edict-drafter, rose to 東閣大學士; **DIES late 1763** (Qianlong 28, 11th month) → alive at the 1763.2.16 start, dies within the game-year = a built-in succession beat. Also **于敏中 Yu Minzhong** (狀元 1737, rising inner-court scholar in 1763, later Chief Grand Councillor) + **劉統勳 Liu Tongxun** (illustrates Study→Council overlap, alive+powerful 1763). Predecessors (dead just before window, flavour only): 汪由敦 d.1758, 張照 d.1745. Elder literary favourite alive 1763: 沈德潛 (~90).
- EN caveat: could not pull the specialist monographs (Wu/Bartlett/Rawski/Elman) as full text — institutional narrative matches their consensus but page-level cites need a print check. English-Wikipedia claim that 南書房 held "highest policy-making power" is an UNCITED overstatement — treat as informal influence only.
