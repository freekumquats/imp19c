# Grand Council Hub + Government-Office Roster — Design & Locked Scope

_Migrated from memory imp19c-grand-council-offices per the research/design-digest-location rule. Design spec — see the memory pointer for status._

Feature requested 2026-07-03 (after the 19-mechanic Qing suite + its GUI hookup shipped): a **Grand Council (軍機處)** mechanic sitting atop a **massive, mostly-abstracted bureaucratic hierarchy**, plus a **greatly expanded roster of character-held government offices**. New offices must influence AND be influenced by existing mechanics (the [[imp19c-qing-mechanics-roadmap]] decline/reform meters) and the new bureaucracy/council layer, with a comprehensive positive/negative event roster. Grounded in cited academic + primary sources (English + Chinese); research kicked off referencing en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Council_(Qing_dynasty).

## LOCKED SCOPE DECISIONS (user, 2026-07-03)

1. **Office model = HYBRID.** The native Imperator office system (`common/offices/00_monarchy.txt`) is GLOBAL to all monarchies (8 offices show for every monarchy — Rome, Egypt, etc.), so a big roster there would break QING-ONLY. Therefore: only 1-2 broadly-useful offices (if any) go native; the distinctly-Qing institutions are a **Qing-only PARALLEL system** (characters + variables + scripted-GUI appointment + custom government-view panel), isolated like the rest of the suite.
2. **Grand Council = CENTRAL HUB mechanic.** Appoint grand councillors (軍機大臣) into seats; Manchu/Han balance matters; chief councillor (領班軍機大臣); council effectiveness computed from seated characters' skills/traits/[[imp19c-qing-mechanics-roadmap]] manchu-identity; effectiveness FEEDS the corruption/reform/ethnic/bureaucracy meters. Rich event chain incl. secrecy, factionalism, a Heshen-style overreach, and the 1911 責任內閣 (responsible cabinet) transition tie-in to the reform track (9b).
3. **Bureaucracy is MOSTLY ABSTRACTED.** The Council sits atop a huge hierarchy modelled as a bureaucracy-capacity/integrity COUNTER layer (NOT per-clerk offices). Fed by corruption, sale-of-offices (捐納, mechanic B), examination-vs-purchase quality, and Council effectiveness.
4. **Only IMPORTANT positions are character-held offices.** Explicitly named by the user: heads of the Six Ministries (六部尚書) and Ambans (駐藏大臣 etc.). Plus (comprehensive scope): Censorate (都察院) head, Lifan Yuan (理藩院), Zongli Yamen (總理衙門, post-1861). Dual Manchu-Han appointment where historical.
5. **Build size = COMPREHENSIVE.** ~5-6 institutions, ~20-30 events, deep cross-wiring. Multi-file suite like the 19-mechanic build.
7. **DIPLOMATIC LAYER added (user, 2026-07-04).** All the internal-governance mechanics must have a FOREIGN-REACTION dimension. Other nations observe + react: (a) WESTERN powers offer advisors/technology/investment in exchange for favours / improved relations / money / concessions (the accept-help-but-incur-dependency tradeoff — historical anchors: Robert Hart & the Imperial Maritime Customs 海關, the Ever-Victorious Army 常勝軍, Beiyang foreign naval advisors, treaty-port loans & railway/mining concessions, the Burlingame Mission 蒲安臣使團 1868); (b) NEIGHBORS & SUBJECTS affected by spillovers, choosing to embrace or resist Qing reform (Korea, Vietnam, Ryukyu, Xinjiang, Tibet). MEIJI JAPAN (明治維新: oyatoi gaikokujin お雇い外国人, Iwakura Mission 岩倉使節団, 富国強兵/文明開化) is the explicit comparative benchmark + rival + alternate-history lever (reform like Meiji → succeed where Self-Strengthening lagged; Japan also the power that shattered it in 1895). Powers react to the player's reform level with approval/rivalry/escalating offers, or predatory concession-grabs if the Qing looks weak. Research agent a3bea2379241dd3f7 running for this layer (EN+ZH+JP sources).

6. **SELF-STRENGTHENING MOVEMENT (自強運動/洋務運動) added (user, 2026-07-04).** A PREEMPTIVELY-pursuable positive path: pursue it early and well → the dynasty enters an alternate-history GOLDEN AGE of successful industrialization + revitalized government (the inverse of the decline cascade). Historically sponsored by Grand Council / governors-general (Prince Gong 恭親王 + Zongli Yamen, Li Hongzhang 李鴻章, Zeng Guofan 曾國藩, Zuo Zongtang 左宗棠), so it CROSS-WIRES to the Grand Council hub and the office holders. Model as a progress track (arsenals 江南製造總局, Fuzhou navy yard 福州船政局, Beiyang fleet 北洋水師, 輪船招商局, 同文館, telegraph/rail) gated by bureaucracy capacity + council effectiveness + treasury; ti-yong framing (中學為體西學為用). Its historical FAILURE modes (conservative opposition, Cixi diverting naval funds to the Summer Palace, no central coordination, defeat 1884-85/1894-95) become the risks a well-played run must overcome. Ties into reform track 9a/9b as the industrial-modernization complement to the political-reform track.

8. **HISTORICAL CHARACTERS spawn anachronistically (user, 2026-07-04).** Real Qing-era figures spawn into the game to play significant roles in events, fill offices/council seats, and influence mechanics — and they may appear ANACHRONISTICALLY (e.g. Sun Yat-sen 孫中山 could show up in 1820). Build a roster of created_character definitions (or spawn effects) with historically-flavoured stats/traits, triggered by the relevant mechanic context rather than by their real birth date. Reformers (Kang Youwei 康有為, Liang Qichao 梁啟超, Sun Yat-sen), self-strengtheners (Li Hongzhang 李鴻章, Zeng Guofan 曾國藩, Zuo Zongtang 左宗棠, Prince Gong 恭親王, Zhang Zhidong 張之洞), councillors/officials (Heshen 和珅, Lin Zexu 林則徐, Zeng/censors), etc. Research agent (see below) to compile the roster with stats + the mechanic each ties to.

9. **MULTIPLE SUCCESSFUL REFORM END-STATES (user, 2026-07-04).** The reform track is NOT just constitutional/republican-or-decline. There are THREE viable SUCCESS tracks, each a real golden-age outcome, not merely survival:
   - **Revitalized ABSOLUTE MONARCHY** — a reformed, industrialized, centralized autocracy (the enlightened-autocrat / high-Qing-restored path; pairs with Self-Strengthening + a strong Grand Council; think a Kangxi/Qianlong-style apex reached anew, or a Meiji-emperor-strong-center analogue). Keeps imperial_monarchy government but with golden-age modifiers.
   - **CONSTITUTIONAL MONARCHY** (君主立憲) — the existing 9b constitutionalist outcome (change_government = constitutional_parliament), matured into a positive golden-age state, not just a pressure-release.
   - **Full REPUBLIC** (共和 / 三民主義) — the existing 9b republican outcome (change_government = constitutional_republic), likewise a genuine successful destination.
   This means reform pressure / self-strengthening progress can resolve UPWARD into any of the three, gated by player choices + faction balance + council/bureaucracy state. Rework the qing_reform.* end to branch into three golden-age resolutions rather than the current binary. Update [[imp19c-qing-mechanics-roadmap]] 9b accordingly.

10. **CHRISTIAN THEOCRACY reform path added (user, 2026-07-04).** A FOURTH successful end-state alongside the three in #9: a **difficult but viable Christian theocracy**. Distinct character:
    - **Hard to succeed** — the highest-risk track; heavy ethnic/sect/legitimacy penalties, conservative + banner backlash, high failure/collapse chance. Not a soft option.
    - **Accelerated by the TAIPING REBELLION event chain** — the Taiping (太平天國, Hong Xiuquan 洪秀全 / Shi Dakai 石達開, God-Worshipping Society 拜上帝會, 天王/翼王, Nanjing→天京 1853) can fire ANACHRONISTICALLY EARLY (ties to the anachronistic-character roster #8 above and [[imp19c-qing-character-roster]]). Historically the Qing's mortal enemy — here the player can instead RIDE it: co-opt/embrace the Christian-millenarian movement to fast-track the theocracy transition rather than suppress it (QING_DECLINE roll already has sect pressure; wire a Taiping branch that can be embraced).
    - **Western support** — choosing the Christian path yields strong WESTERN BACKING (advisors/tech/investment/diplomacy — cross-wires to the diplomatic layer #7: missionary access, sympathetic powers, Ever-Victorious-Army-style aid flips to the player's side, recognition). Historically the West ultimately backed the Qing AGAINST the Taiping; the alternate-history lever is that a credible, governable Christian Qing/successor state instead attracts that support.
    - End-state = a theocratic government form (Christian) with golden-age modifiers if it survives; reworks/extends the qing_reform.* branch and the Self-Strengthening/diplomacy wiring. So the reform track now has FOUR success resolutions: revitalized absolute monarchy, constitutional monarchy, republic, Christian theocracy. Verify available government forms / religion-swap effects in the mod before committing the transition mechanism.

11. **EVENTS TIE TO NAMED BUILDINGS + MILITARY UNITS (user, 2026-07-04).** Every substantive event should have a CONCRETE game-object payload — spawn/grant named buildings and land/naval military units (and treaties) with historical names, not just abstract modifiers. Examples the user gave (illustrative, not exhaustive):
    - **Christian-theocracy path** → spawn a very strong MERCENARY ARMY named the **Eight-Nation Alliance (八國聯軍)** to fight for the player (inversion: historically it crushed the Boxers/Qing in 1900; here it aids the player's Christian Qing).
    - **Modernization / Self-Strengthening** → spawn modern SHIPS as the **Beiyang Fleet (北洋水師)** and **Nanyang Fleet (南洋水師)** (also historically Fujian/Guangdong fleets; Dingyuan 定遠 & Zhenyuan 鎮遠 ironclads as flagship flavour).
    - **War-ending treaties** named after real treaties but with DIFFERENT (often player-favourable, alternate-history) terms — e.g. a **Treaty of Shimonoseki (下關條約/馬關條約)** the Qing dictates rather than suffers. Also Nanjing 南京條約, Tianjin 天津條約, Aigun 璦琿條約, Kyakhta 恰克圖條約 for the Great Game/Russia branch.
    - **Building construction by event**: **railways** (京張鐵路 Jingzhang / 唐胥鐵路 Tangxu), **schools/academies** (同文館 Tongwen Guan, 京師大學堂 → universities, Fuzhou Naval College 福州船政學堂), **factories/arsenals** (江南製造總局 Jiangnan Arsenal, 福州船政局 Fuzhou Navy Yard, 漢陽鐵廠 Hanyang Ironworks, 開平煤礦 Kaiping Mines, 輪船招商局 China Merchants SNC).
    Implementation: use `create_unit`/`spawn_unit`-style effects for armies/fleets (verify exact effect + how to name a unit/legion), `add_building`/province-building effects for structures (verify against the mod's building defs + economy/industrialization layer per constraint above), and government/peace effects for named treaties. All must cross-wire to the economy/industry/currency sim (a spawned arsenal = real industry; a fleet = real navy; a railway = real infra/commerce), and be historically referenced in loc. VERIFY each object exists in the mod's defs (buildings, unit/legion types, mercenary mechanics) before referencing — the mod is a total conversion, so vanilla Imperator building/unit keys may not apply.
